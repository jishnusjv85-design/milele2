Frequently asked questions

Can I confirm exact material and stone details?

Yes. Ask from the product page or WhatsApp and the available specification can be confirmed before order.

Can I enquire without adding to cart?

Yes. Every product detail page includes a direct WhatsApp enquiry option.

How should I care for my jewellery?

Keep pieces dry and stored separately, and avoid direct contact with fragrance, chlorine and harsh chemicals. See the Care & Authenticity page for more.
