Ownership, made considered

Care, clarity and confidence.

Every order should begin with a clear understanding of the piece you are choosing and how to care for it over time.

01

Product specification

Before your order is confirmed, our team can verify the available specification for the selected piece—including material, stone details, dimensions and any product-specific care requirements.

02

Everyday care

Store jewellery separately in a dry, soft-lined space. Avoid direct contact with perfume, cosmetics, chlorine and harsh cleaning chemicals, and wipe gently after wear.

03

After-purchase support

If a piece needs inspection, cleaning guidance or service, contact Milèle with your order details. The team will advise the appropriate next step based on the design and condition.

04

Order & delivery clarity

Availability, final delivery timing, shipping method and any piece-specific conditions are confirmed with you before dispatch so there are no assumptions around a high-value order.
